# VibeTravels MVP and Success Criteria Overview

## 🧭 Main Problem
Planning engaging and interesting trips is challenging. By leveraging the potential, creativity, and knowledge of AI, VibeTravels allows you to transform simplified notes about destinations and travel goals into concrete itineraries.

## 🛠️ Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
- Saving, reading, browsing, and deleting notes about future trips  
- A simple user account system to link users with their notes  
- A user profile page for storing travel preferences  
- AI integration to convert notes into detailed plans, taking into account preferences, time, number of people, and potential places and attractions

## 🚫 What’s NOT Included in the MVP
- Sharing trip plans between accounts  
- Rich multimedia support and analysis (e.g., photos of places to visit)  
- Advanced time and logistics planning

## ✅ Success Criteria
- 90% of users have filled out their travel preferences in their profile  
- 75% of users generate 3 or more trip plans per year
